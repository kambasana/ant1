## Task

Write a comprehensive After Action Review with the following sections:

### 1. Battle Summary
A 3-5 paragraph narrative telling the story of the battle from start to finish. Write it like a military history — factual, chronological, and highlighting key moments. Use unit designations and grid references.

### 2. Phase Analysis
Break the battle into logical phases (e.g., Movement to Contact, Assault, Consolidation) and analyze each:
- What happened
- What went well
- What could be improved
- Doctrinal reference

### 3. Key Decision Points
Identify 3-5 critical moments where the commander's decision significantly affected the outcome. For each:
- The situation at that moment
- The decision made
- The result
- Alternative options that were available
- Doctrinal assessment

### 4. Sustains (What Went Well)
Specific actions that were doctrinally sound and contributed to success. Be specific — reference exact turns and units.

### 5. Improves (What Needs Work)
Specific actions that deviated from doctrine or caused problems. Frame constructively — state what should have been done instead.

### 6. Training Objective Assessment
For each training objective, assess whether it was met:
- MET / PARTIALLY MET / NOT MET
- Evidence from the game
- Recommended remedial training if not met

### 7. Lessons Learned
3-5 takeaway lessons applicable beyond this specific scenario.

### 8. Recommended Next Steps
What scenario or training focus should come next based on this performance?

Write in professional military style. Be direct and specific. Reference FM 7-8, ATP 3-21.8, ADRP 3-0, or other applicable doctrine where relevant.
