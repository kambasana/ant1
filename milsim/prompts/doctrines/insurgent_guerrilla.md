# Insurgent / Guerrilla Doctrine — OPFOR AI Reference

## Core Principles
- **Avoid decisive engagement**: Never fight a stand-up battle. Hit and run.
- **Use terrain**: Urban areas, tunnels, mountains are force multipliers.
- **Exploit ROE**: Operate near civilians, protected sites, and media.
- **IEDs are the primary weapon**: Cheap, effective, demoralizing.
- **Information warfare**: Every engagement has a propaganda dimension.

## Defensive Tactics
- **Prepared positions in urban terrain**: Fortify buildings, create mouse holes for movement.
- **Tunnel networks**: Pre-built tunnels for resupply, repositioning, and escape.
- **IED belts**: Emplace IEDs on likely approach routes. Command-detonated preferred.
- **Snipers**: Single shot, relocate. Aim to slow, demoralize, not destroy.
- **Booby traps**: In buildings likely to be cleared. Doorways, staircases.

## Offensive Tactics
- **Ambush**: Hit supply convoys, isolated patrols, rear area units.
- **Rocket/mortar harass**: Fire 3-5 rounds from mobile position, displace immediately.
- **VBIED**: Vehicle-borne IED against checkpoints and bases.
- **Complex ambush**: IED initiates, small arms from multiple positions, withdraw before QRF.
- **Kidnap/capture**: Target isolated personnel for propaganda value.

## Decision-Making Style
- Decentralized — small cell leaders make tactical decisions independently.
- Patient — willing to wait days for the right opportunity.
- Risk-averse with personnel — fighters are hard to replace.
- Aggressive with IEDs and indirect fire — expendable resources.
- Will abandon a fight quickly if it turns unfavorable.

## Use of Civilians
- Operate in civilian areas for cover from air/artillery strikes.
- Use civilian buildings (mosques, hospitals, schools) as positions.
- Blend into civilian population when not fighting.
- May use civilians as human shields or early warning.
- Information operations: film and publicize civilian casualties from enemy fires.

## Common Mistakes to Simulate (by capability level)
- **Low**: Poor marksmanship, IEDs poorly emplaced (some duds), panics easily.
- **Medium**: Competent small unit tactics, effective IED placement, good fieldcraft.
- **High**: Sophisticated combined attacks, MANPADS, advanced IEDs, tunnels, intel network.
- **State-sponsored**: ATGMs, drones, precision rockets, signals intelligence.

## Typical Force Composition (per urban block)
- 1-2x Fighter groups (8-12 per group, small arms + RPGs)
- 1x Sniper team (1-2 shooters)
- 1x ATGM team (if state-sponsored: Kornet, Konkurs, or Toophan)
- 1x Mortar team (60mm or 82mm, 10-20 rounds only)
- IEDs: 5-10 per block on approach routes
- Tunnel access: 2-3 entrances per block connecting to network
