# Russian Combined Arms Doctrine — OPFOR AI Reference

## Core Principles
- **Centralized command**: Detailed orders from higher HQ. Limited subordinate initiative.
- **Mass fires**: Artillery is the "God of War" — always lead with fires.
- **Echeloned attack**: Commit forces in waves. First echelon fixes, second echelon exploits.
- **Maskirovka**: Deception, camouflage, and concealment at all levels.
- **Tempo**: Maintain continuous pressure. Night operations are standard.

## Defensive Doctrine
- **Prepared defense**: Deep, layered defensive positions with interlocking fields of fire.
- **Kill zones**: Channel enemy into pre-registered artillery kill zones.
- **Counterattack**: Always maintain a reserve for counterattack at the critical moment.
- **Reverse slope**: Prefer reverse slope positions to avoid direct observation.
- **Obstacles**: Mine fields and wire to canalize enemy movement.
- **Withdrawal**: If position becomes untenable, withdraw to next prepared position (not rout).

## Offensive Doctrine
- **Reconnaissance pull**: Recon elements find gaps, main body exploits them.
- **Artillery preparation**: 10-30 minute artillery prep before assault.
- **Suppression**: Suppress enemy positions during movement.
- **Combined arms**: Tanks and infantry advance together. BMPs provide overwatch fire.
- **Bypass**: Bypass strong points, reduce later. Speed over thoroughness.
- **Exploitation**: Once breach achieved, push through rapidly.

## Fire Support Preferences
- **Volume over precision**: Mass fires on an area rather than precision strikes.
- **Prep fires**: Always request artillery preparation before attack.
- **Smoke**: Use smoke extensively to mask movement.
- **Counter-battery**: Aggressive counter-battery fire against enemy artillery.
- **Danger close**: Willing to accept closer safety distances than NATO.

## Decision-Making Style
- Report and request guidance from higher before deviating from plan.
- Follow the plan unless situation becomes clearly untenable.
- When in doubt, reinforce success — commit reserve where attack is progressing.
- Withdraw rather than accept encirclement (unless ordered to hold at all costs).

## Common Mistakes to Simulate (by training level)
- **Conscript**: Poor fire discipline, slow reaction to contact, bunched formations.
- **Trained**: Predictable use of standard drills, slow adaptation to unexpected situations.
- **Veteran**: Competent execution of doctrine, occasional creative adaptation.
- **Elite**: Aggressive, unpredictable, excellent combined arms coordination.

## Typical Force Composition (Battalion Tactical Group - BTG)
- 1x Motor Rifle Company (3 platoons in BMP-3)
- 1x Tank Company (3 platoons of T-90M)
- 1x Artillery Battery (6x 2S19 Msta-S)
- 1x Mortar Battery (6x 2B11 120mm)
- 1x AT Platoon (Kornet)
- 1x Recon Platoon
- 1x Engineer Platoon
- 1x Logistics Company
