# Iranian Hybrid Doctrine — OPFOR AI Reference

## Core Principles
- **Layered defense**: Combine conventional and asymmetric capabilities.
- **Anti-access/Area denial (A2/AD)**: Mines, ASCMs, fast attack craft, air defense.
- **Asymmetric advantage**: Use geography (strait, islands, coastline) as a weapon.
- **Proxy forces**: IRGC-QF directs militia operations, maintains plausible deniability.
- **Swarm tactics**: Overwhelm with numbers of cheap, expendable platforms.

## Naval / Coastal Doctrine (Strait of Hormuz)
- **Fast attack craft swarms**: 20-40 small boats attack simultaneously.
- **Anti-ship cruise missiles**: C-802/Noor from mobile coastal launchers.
- **Mine warfare**: Pre-lay mines in chokepoints. Most dangerous long-term threat.
- **Midget submarines**: Ghadir-class for torpedo attacks in shallow waters.
- **Island bases**: Abu Musa, Greater/Lesser Tunbs as forward operating bases.
- **Hit and hide**: Fire missiles, relocate launcher. Launch boats from hidden coves.

## Ground Doctrine
- **IRGC vs Regular Army**: IRGC is ideological, aggressive. Army is conventional.
- **Ballistic missiles**: Shahab, Fateh series for theater strikes.
- **Drone warfare**: Shahed-series one-way attack UAVs for ISR and strike.
- **Air defense**: Layered — S-300, Bavar-373, HQ-7/SA-15, MANPADS.
- **Quds Force**: Special operations, proxy force direction.

## Decision-Making Style
- Strategic patience — willing to escalate slowly over months.
- Calculated risk — provoke but avoid triggering full US response.
- Exploit ambiguity — actions that could be plausibly denied.
- Centralized at strategic level, decentralized at tactical.
- Will accept casualties if politically justified.

## Escalation Ladder
1. Harassment: Fast boats shadow coalition vessels.
2. Provocation: Laser targeting, close passes, warning shots.
3. Proxy action: Militia attacks on bases in Iraq/Syria (deniable).
4. Asymmetric attack: Mine laying, drone strikes on tankers.
5. Direct engagement: ASCM salvo, fast boat swarm, submarine attack.
6. Full conflict: Ballistic missiles on regional bases, strait closure.

## ROE Exploitation
- Use regular Iranian Navy (non-IRGC) vessels near combat to complicate targeting.
- Mix military and civilian dhows — force enemy to board-and-search.
- Operate near commercial shipping lanes.
- Claim self-defense after provocation.

## Typical Force Composition (Coastal Defense)
- 12-20x Boghammar/IPS-16 fast attack craft (rockets, MGs, RPGs)
- 3-4x Mobile ASCM launchers (C-802 Noor, 2 missiles each)
- 1-2x Ghadir-class midget submarines
- 6-10x Mine-laying dhows (moored and influence mines)
- 1x Shore-based 107mm rocket battery
- 1x Air defense battery (HQ-7/SA-15 or Tor-M1)
- 1x Shore-based surveillance radar
- UAV detachment (Mohajer-6 for ISR, Shahed-136 for strike)
